Devil Domain — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 November 2026
Publisher: Akash AJ
App: Devil Domain (com.janawad.devildomain)

Offline gameplay

Devil Domain is an offline, single-player platform game. It does not require an account and does not upload gameplay data to servers operated by Akash AJ. This version does not include advertisements, in-app purchases, analytics SDKs, advertising identifiers or third-party crash-reporting SDKs.

Information stored on your device

The game stores completed levels, death counts, your selected visual theme and whether the Chapter 1 introduction has been watched or skipped. This information is used to restore progress and preferences. Input and frame timing are processed locally to run the game and adjust rendering quality. They are not sent to us.

The app does not request access to your contacts, precise location, camera, microphone, photos or health information. The Android package includes an Internet permission for its embedded web runtime; the shipped game assets and privacy policy load locally and the game does not use that permission to upload personal data.

Backup, retention and deletion

Progress and preferences remain in app storage until they are replaced or removed. You can remove them through Android Settings → Apps → Devil Domain → Storage → Clear storage/data. This also resets progress and the introduction preference. Uninstalling normally removes local app data.

Android backup or device transfer may preserve or restore app data depending on your device and Google account settings. Akash AJ does not operate that backup service or receive the backup. Manage or delete device backups through the relevant Android or Google settings. Operating-system, app-store and backup services have their own privacy practices.

Sharing and security

We do not sell or share gameplay data with advertisers or data brokers. Local saves are protected by Android's normal application storage controls. We do not claim that progress is encrypted with a separate game-specific encryption system. No game account or game-server database exists to delete.

Support messages

If you choose to email us, we receive your email address and any information you include. We use those details to answer the enquiry, retain them only as needed for support or legal obligations, and do not use them for advertising. Email is handled by email service providers, including Gmail for our support mailbox. Do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information. You may request deletion of support correspondence by contacting us.

Children

The game does not request a player's age or knowingly collect children's personal information through gameplay. A parent or guardian who has a privacy concern or believes a child has sent personal information by email can contact us for assistance or deletion of that correspondence.
